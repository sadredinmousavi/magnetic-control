## Scaling estimate for the characteristic inter-robot spacing

To estimate the characteristic separation between neighboring microrobots, we compare the magnitude of the pairwise magnetic repulsion with the characteristic magnetic actuation force generated by the external permanent magnets.

Assuming vertical magnetic moments and planar motion, the repulsive dipole–dipole force between two identical microrobots separated by a center-to-center distance \(d\) scales as

\[
F_{rr}(d)
=
\frac{3\mu_0}{4\pi}
\frac{m_r^2}{d^4},
\]

where \(m_r\) is the magnetic moment of one microrobot. The magnetic actuation force exerted on one microrobot by a representative external permanent magnet at distance \(R_s\) is

\[
F_{PM}
=
\frac{3\mu_0}{4\pi}
\frac{m_s m_r}{R_s^4},
\]

where \(m_s\) is the magnetic moment of the source magnet.

The characteristic spacing \(d^\ast\) can be estimated by balancing these two force scales:

\[
F_{rr}(d^\ast) \sim F_{PM}.
\]

Thus,

\[
\frac{3\mu_0}{4\pi}
\frac{m_r^2}{(d^\ast)^4}
\sim
\frac{3\mu_0}{4\pi}
\frac{m_s m_r}{R_s^4}.
\]

After cancellation,

\[
\frac{m_r}{(d^\ast)^4}
\sim
\frac{m_s}{R_s^4},
\]

which gives

\[
d^\ast
\sim
R_s
\left(
\frac{m_r}{m_s}
\right)^{1/4}.
\]

For the present system,

\[
m_s = M L_s^3,
\qquad
m_r = M L_r^3.
\]

Using

\[
M=868\times10^3\ \mathrm{A/m},
\]

\[
L_s=20\ \mathrm{mm},
\]

\[
L_r=0.5\ \mathrm{mm},
\]

and

\[
R_s=250\ \mathrm{mm},
\]

we obtain

\[
m_s = 6.94\ \mathrm{A\,m^2},
\]

\[
m_r = 1.09\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{A\,m^2}.
\]

Therefore,

\[
d^\ast
\sim
0.25
\left(
\frac{1.09\times10^{-4}}{6.94}
\right)^{1/4}
\]

\[
d^\ast
\approx
1.58\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{m}.
\]

Hence,

\[
\boxed{
d^\ast \approx 16\ \mathrm{mm}
}
\]

This estimate indicates that, for the chosen magnetic moments and actuation distance, the inter-robot magnetic repulsion becomes comparable to the characteristic external actuation force at a center-to-center distance of approximately \(15\)–\(20\ \mathrm{mm}\). Therefore, in the absence of strong confinement or additional attractive interactions, the microrobots are expected to maintain separations on the order of \(10\ \mathrm{mm}\).
